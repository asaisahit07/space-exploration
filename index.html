🌌 Unknown Facts

🕳️ Black Holes

Black holes are regions where gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape.

🌀 Wormholes

Wormholes are theoretical shortcuts through space-time that could connect distant regions of the universe.

🌑 Dark Matter

Scientists believe dark matter makes up most of the matter in the universe, yet nobody knows exactly what it is.

⚡ Dark Energy

Dark energy is causing the universe to expand faster and faster.

⭐ Neutron Stars

A teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh billions of tons on Earth.
